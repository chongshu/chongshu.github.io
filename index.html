I am an Assistant Professor of Finance at the David Eccles School of Business, University of Utah. I received my PhD in Finance from University of Southern California in 2021. My research examines corporate governance, shareholder voting, and systemic risk — with particular focus on how institutional investors and proxy advisors shape corporate outcomes.

My work is published in the Journal of Financial Economics and Management Science. It has informed policy: cited by the SEC, referenced in congressional investigations, and quoted in federal court briefs. Media coverage includes Reuters, Forbes, and The Economist.
